About

A fresh approach to an age-old craft

Sears Melvin Memorials was founded with a simple belief: families deserve better. Better communication, better care, and a clear picture of what happens next.

We are a young team in North London, working with families across the UK. The showroom is open by appointment. We design and install headstones, kerb sets and other memorials, and we handle the cemetery permit application for you.

How we help

From a conversation to the graveside

No obligation at the start. From order to installation is typically 12–18 weeks. The main variable is permit approval, which differs by cemetery.

  1. 1Consultation

    Talk through what you want — at the showroom, on the phone, or by video. Same day, when we can.

  2. 2Design and quote

    A written proposal with clear pricing, usually within a day or two. Take the time you need.

  3. 3Permit and making

    We apply to the cemetery and keep you posted while the memorial is made. Often 10–16 weeks.

  4. 4Installation

    Fitted with care, with photos when it is done. A 30-year guarantee comes with the memorial.
