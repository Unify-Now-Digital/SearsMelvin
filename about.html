About us

About Sears Melvin

North London memorial masons. Traditional craftsmanship, honest guidance.

Sears Melvin Memorials is a North London memorial mason. We provide and install headstones, kerb-set monuments and gravestone renovations, and we look after the paperwork in between, including cemetery permits, so families have one calm point of contact from first conversation to installation.

Our story

How we work

We work in the traditional way, with stone carved and fixed by qualified masons. We also keep things clear: clear guide prices online, honest advice about what each cemetery allows, and taking the time to listen before suggesting anything.

Every memorial starts with a conversation. Whether you visit our showroom, call us or send a message online, we will listen to your wishes and guide you through the options.
