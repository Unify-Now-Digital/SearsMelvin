11

Photo frames

Memorial photos sit on white, with 2px corners. Do not tint the stone. Do not crop it with a curved top. Use object-fit: contain.

Square white well for a product card. 2px corners. The approved photography standard below is a 4:5 frame on linen.
12

Photography

Approved. The standard is a 4:5 frame on linen. Product cards still use the white photo well until a later phase.

80% of the frame height

Use a 4:5 frame. The ground is linen, token --sm-color-bg.

Centre the stone on a common baseline. The stone is about 80% of the frame height. Add a soft ground shadow.

Keep the whole memorial in the frame. A wide kerb set needs more side space. Keep the stone colour, the shape, and the surface. Clean the background only. Do not change the camera angle.
