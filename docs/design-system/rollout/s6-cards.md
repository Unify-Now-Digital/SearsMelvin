# S6 cards

Stage S6 sets the memorial card and the homepage category card.
The photo well is white and the picture uses `object-fit: contain`.
The info panel is sand, with `--sm-space-4` padding.
The corner is 2px. The edge is 1px sand.
The shadow is `--sm-shadow-sm`. Hover uses `--sm-shadow-md` and does not lift the card.
There is no arched top and no zoom on the photo.

The title is Cormorant 500 at `--sm-text-card-title` (24px), in ink.
The category label is ink on sand.
The price stays on the S4 price rules. A category price uses the same DM Sans price token.

The card remains one link. `href`, `data-id`, `data-slug`, `data-name`, `data-price-value`, and `data-type` are unchanged.
The heart stays a separate `save-btn`, 44px square, with a 2px corner, a white ground and a bronze edge. The saved heart stays ink with a linen mark.
`shortlist-btn-` classes are unchanged.
